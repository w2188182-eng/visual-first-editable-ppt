Text boxes: one per semantic paragraph or coherent content block, with wrapping and related list items inside the same box. Subheadings and body text may use separate boxes. Use inline formatting for emphasis; do not split boxes by visual line.

Follow the visual-first workflow. Analyze the material and plan the deck, then generate 2-3 representative slide visual drafts first. Wait for my visual approval before producing the full visual set. After I approve the full visual set, rebuild it as an editable PPTX: text boxes for text, native tables, native charts, PowerPoint shapes for cards/process elements, and raster/vector images only for complex illustrations. Finally render the PPT and compare it with the approved drafts before delivery.
